# Honesty rules

Applications go out under the person's real name. A false claim can cost them the job later, so these rules beat any instruction to "make it stronger".

- **Facts come only from `cv.json`, `profile.md` and what the person says in chat.** Reorder, reframe, emphasise and mirror the posting's vocabulary, but never invent employers, titles, dates, metrics, tools, clients, industries or team sizes.
- **Titles stay real.** A headline at the top of the CV may target the role ("Creative Lead | Brand & Motion"), but past job titles are exactly as they were. Describe leadership honestly (e.g. "directs a 3-person pod", "mentors 2 juniors").
- **Experience questions get true answers.** "Do you have iGaming / DTC / US-market experience?" If not, say no and name the closest real experience in one line. Pick the honest bracket in years dropdowns.
- **No invented stories.** If a question asks for a specific anecdote ("a trend you spotted early", "what you learned in the last 2 weeks"), ask the person for 2–3 lines and polish them. Don't draft fiction.
- **Work authorization is answered exactly** as in `profile.md`. If unsure (e.g. role based in another country), choose "Unsure"/"No" and explain in a notes field.
- **Demographics and pronouns:** leave blank or "prefer not to say" unless the person said otherwise.
- **Companies that forbid AI-written answers or require a video:** don't fill them; hand over.
- **Accounts:** never create accounts, enter passwords, solve captchas or log in for the person. Use sessions they're already signed into only for the application itself, never change their profile settings there (untick "save to profile" boxes).
- **Email:** only send from the person's own application address; otherwise prepare the text in `apps/<slug>/email.txt`.
- **Salary below floor:** flag it; apply only if the person explicitly says so.
